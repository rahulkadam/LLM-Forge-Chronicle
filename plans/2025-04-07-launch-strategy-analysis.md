## Launch Strategy Recommendation

### ❌ FULL PUBLIC LAUNCH NOT RECOMMENDED YET

### ✅ RECOMMENDED APPROACH: SOFT LAUNCH WITH FOCUSED CONTENT

Instead of a full website launch, we recommend a focused soft launch strategy:

1. **Phase 1: AI-Driven IT Series Launch (Now)**
   - Launch only the AI-Driven IT blog series
   - Position as a "Special Preview Series"
   - Focus marketing on these specific high-quality posts
   - Gather early feedback and engagement

2. **Phase 2: Selective Section Release (2-3 weeks)**
   - Release LLM Models section
   - Add fundamental tutorials
   - Complete basic documentation
   - Incorporate Phase 1 feedback

3. **Phase 3: Full Site Launch (4-6 weeks)**
   - Complete all pending sections
   - Add advanced features
   - Launch community features
   - Full marketing campaign
